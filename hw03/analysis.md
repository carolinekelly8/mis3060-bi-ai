# HW3 Analysis — Do Executive Changes Precede or Follow Earnings?

Across all five companies, most executive events came before the nearest earnings announcement: 22 of 34 events were filed before earnings, 10 after, and 2 in the same week. Walmart shows the clearest pattern, with 8 events before earnings and 2 in the same week, including a group of leadership changes filed on January 16, 2026, about five weeks before its February earnings release. Microsoft is the exception, with 3 of its 4 events filed after earnings, while Apple, NVIDIA and JPMorgan are mixed but lean toward "before." One possible explanation is that companies prefer to announce leadership changes ahead of earnings so the new team can be introduced on the earnings call. However, the sample is small, and many rows are unnamed filings about compensation or board matters, so this is a tendency in our data rather than a proven pattern.
